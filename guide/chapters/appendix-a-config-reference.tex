\chapter{Configuration Reference}
\label{ch:config-reference}

All settings are defined in \codefile{apps/api/config.py} as a Pydantic
\ic{BaseSettings} class. Every field maps to an environment variable of the
same name (upper-cased). Place overrides in \codefile{.env} in the repository
root or export them in your shell.

% ── Database ──────────────────────────────────────────────
\multicolumn{5}{l}{\textbf{Database}} \\
\midrule
\confkey{database\_url} & \ic{str} & \ic{""} & \ic{DATABASE\_URL} &
  PostgreSQL async connection string. Auto-converts \ic{postgresql://} to
  \ic{postgresql+asyncpg://}. \\
\confkey{neo4j\_uri} & \ic{str} & \ic{""} & \ic{NEO4J\_URI} &
  Neo4j Bolt or \ic{neo4j+s://} URI. \\
\confkey{neo4j\_user} & \ic{str} & \ic{""} & \ic{NEO4J\_USER} &
  Neo4j username. \\
\confkey{neo4j\_password} & \ic{SecretStr} & \ic{""} & \ic{NEO4J\_PASSWORD} &
  Neo4j password. Masked in logs. \\
\confkey{redis\_url} & \ic{str} & \ic{""} & \ic{REDIS\_URL} &
  Redis connection string with optional password. \\

% ── LLM Provider ─────────────────────────────────────────
\midrule
\multicolumn{5}{l}{\textbf{LLM Provider}} \\
\midrule
\confkey{llm\_provider} & \ic{str} & \ic{"nvidia"} & \ic{LLM\_PROVIDER} &
  Active LLM backend: \ic{"nvidia"} or \ic{"bedrock"}. \\
\confkey{embedding\_provider} & \ic{str} & \ic{"nvidia"} & \ic{EMBEDDING\_PROVIDER} &
  Embedding backend. Keep \ic{"nvidia"} even with Bedrock LLM to avoid
  re-indexing. \\

\begin{keyinsight}
Settings whose type is \ic{SecretStr} (\confkey{neo4j\_password},
\confkey{nvidia\_api\_key}, \confkey{nvidia\_embedding\_api\_key},
\confkey{secret\_key}) are automatically masked in log output and
\ic{repr()} calls. Access their values through the corresponding getter
methods, e.g.\ \ic{settings.get\_nvidia\_api\_key()}.
\end{keyinsight}
